# Day Nineteen

## Conceptos básicos referenciados en este capítulo

- [[caridad]] — amor no egoísta sino generoso

## Día diecinueve

Sheen enseña: «Love is the key to the mystery. Love by its very nature is not selfish, but generous. It seeks not its own, but the good of others. The measure of love is not the pleasure it gives — that is the way the world judges it — but the joy and peace it can purchase for others». El devoto reconoce el eco de san Pablo y pide ayuda para amar a Dios y a los demás y cumplir la regla de oro.
