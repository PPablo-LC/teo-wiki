## Conceptos básicos referenciados en este capítulo

## Día nueve

Sheen afirma: «Believe the incredible, and you can do the impossible. It is our want of faith that holds us back, even as Peter. When did he begin to sink? The Gospel gives us the reason. He took account of the winds, he began reading some surveys; it was established statistically that 99.44% of mankind cannot walk on water. All of the incredulities were in the winds. When he took his eyes off Christ, Peter began to sink». El devoto pide a Sheen que siga mostrándole a Cristo en todo momento, para que, aun cuando su petición requiera un milagro, recuerde la promesa de que Jesús es el Dios de lo milagroso.
