## Día veinticuatro

Sheen afirma: «We must go out to Pure Life, Pure Truth, Pure Love, and that is the definition of God. He is the ultimate goal of life; from Him we came, and in Him alone do we find our peace». El devoto reconoce que Dios es el Amor mismo y pide que siga impulsándolo cada vez más cerca del Amor infinito.
